Privacy Policy

Run It Back · Charles Chen LLC · Effective October 21, 2026

Run It Back is a basketball life sim you play in your browser or in our app. Charles Chen LLC ("we") makes it. This page says what the game stores, what it sends, and how to turn the sending off.

What stays on your device

Your saved game (your player, your choices, your season) is stored on your device: in your browser's local storage on the web, or in the app's storage on a phone. It is never sent to us. If you clear your browser's site data, delete the app, or use a private window, the save is gone and we can't restore it.

Safari on iPhone and iPad deletes a website's stored data after 7 days without a visit. Adding Run It Back to your Home Screen keeps your save.

The name you type for your player stays on your device. It is never sent.

Play data we collect

Unless you turn it off, the game sends play data to Amplitude, the analytics service we use. We use it to see where players stop, which choices they make, and how often luck decides a result, so we can tune the game. Play data includes:

  • a random ID created on your device the first time you visit this site or play, plus a session ID and the time of each event
  • on this site's home page: that you opened it, which site linked you there (such as google.com), and which Play button you tapped
  • the game version, whether you're on the web, iPhone or Android, and which build you're on
  • what happens in the game: weeks started and finished, choices and their results, games played and box scores, split-second plays, injuries, episodes started and finished, and how long you were away between visits
  • a snapshot of your season at the end of each week: your ratings, your standing with each character, your record, and the dice rolls that decided results
  • if an ad or a tagged link brought you to the site, which one: its campaign tags (such as utm_source and utm_campaign) and, for a Google ad, Google's click ID. Only the first one counts, so the site keeps it in your browser's local storage

The game doesn't collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, advertising ID or precise location. There are no accounts and no ads. Buying something is the one exception; see Purchases below.

Like any website, Amplitude receives your IP address when the game sends data, and it may use the IP address to estimate your country, region and city.

Purchases

Sophomore year, and skipping the wait between episodes, are sold through Stripe's checkout on the web, with Link (a Stripe service) as the seller of record. Stripe collects your email, payment details and billing address to take the payment, work out sales tax and send your receipt; see Stripe's privacy policy. We never see your full card number.

Checkout also receives the random ID from your device, so a payment can be matched to the device that made it. Stripe shows us your email, the amount, your country and that ID. If you buy, your play data can therefore be linked to your email. We use that only to deliver what you bought, answer support requests and give refunds.

In the iPhone app, purchases go through Apple's App Store instead. Apple takes the payment and sends your receipt, and we never see your payment details. The app uses RevenueCat, a purchase service, to confirm each purchase with Apple and to restore purchases on a new device. RevenueCat receives the random ID from your device and what you bought, and shows us the same.

Your device remembers the purchase in local storage. If that is cleared, your receipt email is your proof of purchase. Email us with it and we'll make sure you get what you paid for.

How to turn it off

Open the menu on the home screen (the button with three lines) and tap "Anonymous play data" until it reads off. The game stops sending play data and deletes any play data still waiting on your device to be sent, and the home page stops sending visits and taps. Turn it back on the same way.

Who else gets it

Amplitude stores and processes play data for us. We don't sell play data or use it to show you ads. We would disclose it if the law required us to.

If a Google ad brought you to the site and you then start a career, we tell Google Ads the ad's click ID and when the career started. Google uses that to report which ads bring players and to decide which searches to show our ads on; see Google's privacy policy. We send Google nothing else about you or your game. If play data is off, we never learn that the career started, so nothing is sent.

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs the IP addresses of visitors for security; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Cookies

The game sets no cookies. It uses your browser's local storage for your save, your play data setting, play data waiting to be sent, and the ad or link that first brought you.

How long we keep it

We keep play data as long as it helps us tune the game. Play data is tied to a random ID, not to you, so unless you've bought something we can't look it up by your name or email. If you want the data from your device deleted, email us and we'll work out with you how to find it.

Children

Run It Back is made for players 13 and older. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has sent us information, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change what the game collects, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
