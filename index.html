How it plays

One week at a time.

Every week runs the same four steps.

1

Plan

Four blocks of free time after class, practice and games. Fill them with skill work, open gym, film, lifting, conditioning, study, family, friends, rest or free time. Stack too much hoops in one week and the third and fourth blocks count for less.

2

Live it

One or two scenes a week with your coach, your teammates, your friends and your family. You make the call, and people remember it.

3

Play

Games go possession by possession for 32 minutes. Coach decides your minutes. When the game stops on you, it's your call.

4

See what changed

The recap shows what got better and where it came from, how your body's holding up, where you stand with people, and what's coming next week.

Game night

The game stops on you.

Twice a game, three times in tournament and rival games, the clock freezes with the scoreboard up and it's on you. Make the sound play, gamble, stay out of it, or take over.

About once every two games you get a split-second play: a loose ball, a charge to take, a layup to stop, a box-out. Go physical and there's a small chance you get hurt.

After the buzzer you get the box score. Your minutes, buckets, boards and dimes go in a game log you keep all year.

The season

Make the list. Then earn your minutes.

Tryouts are in November. Fifty-one kids, thirty spots.

Make the freshman team and you're still fighting for minutes. Coach Nakamura reviews roles three times a season. Get cut and the year keeps going, and so does your shot at a spot. Ball out and you could get called up. JV is a long shot. Varsity is a really long shot.

Sixteen games: six non-league, then ten league games against five schools, twice each. Then all six league teams go into one bracket. Lose once and you're done.

The other bench

Eleven aces. One remembers you.

Every team you play has an ace with his own game. Watch film or run into him once and you learn the counter. Micah Reyes is your season rival. He goes at your weak hand, and he remembers what you did last time.

Your people

Nobody shows you a meter.

People tell you where you stand in words. They remember what you did, and they drift if you go weeks without showing up. Stay close and it pays off: Jonathan's workouts get better, and Gordon starts looking for you on the floor.

Get called up and you meet Coach Brennan, who runs JV and talks trash with his players, and Coach Dunbar, who runs varsity and wants to know what you saw before you passed.

Under the hood

Work, luck and a growth spurt.

The game tracks eight skills: shooting, handle, passing, defense, basketball IQ, strength, speed and explosiveness. It also tracks confidence, discipline, grades, fatigue and your body.

Growth

One growth roll a year

You grow one to five inches this year, and more is likely if your parents' heights say you've got more coming. The spurt starts somewhere between week 4 and week 26 and takes three to seven weeks to show up.

Training

Put in the work

A normal week's gains swing up to 35% either way. Once in a while you hit a breakthrough week worth up to nine normal ones. Rarer still, a week gives you almost nothing. Game minutes and good coaching stack on top.

Fatigue

Cooked legs cost you

Run yourself into the ground and your gains shrink and your injury risk climbs. A rest block takes fatigue off. The plan shows the risk of each block before you lock it in.

Injuries

Tweaks happen

Practice, workouts and games carry a small chance of getting hurt, and nothing makes it zero. No injury lasts more than two weeks or costs more than four games.

Minutes

Coach decides

Being healthy and ready doesn't promise minutes. Nakamura never sees your real ratings, only what you show him, and he reviews roles in weeks 16, 22 and 27.

Seeds

Same seed, same year

Every year runs from a seed number. Same number, same profile, same choices, same year. Change one choice and run it back.

Is it free?

Freshman year is free, all eight episodes. After each episode the next one unlocks an hour later. On the web you can pay $1 to skip the wait.

Is there more after freshman year?

Sophomore year is in the works. Finish freshman year and you can pre-order it for $5. It'll be $8 when it drops.

Where is my save?

In your browser, on your device. There's no account, so clearing your browser's site data or playing in a private window loses it. Safari on iPhone and iPad deletes a site's data after 7 days without a visit.

Is there an app?

An iPhone app is on the way. For now, play in your browser on your phone or computer.

Can I rename people?

You name your own player. You can also rename Gordon, Jonathan and Christina.

What do you collect?

Anonymous play data: which choices and results happen, never your name. You can turn it off from the menu on the game's home screen. The privacy policy has the details.
